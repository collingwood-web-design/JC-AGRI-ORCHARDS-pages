The orchard is located in the heart of Nottawa, 10 min south of Collingwood just off of Hwy 124. NEW ENTRANCE on Batteaux Rd.

Admission $2.00 per person. Children under 2 free. Apples: $2.35 /lb.

This Thanksgiving weekend we will be hosting our annual Donation Weekend. All apple proceeds will be donated to local food banks in our area. Excluded: Apple Cider, Baked Goods and Honey.

We are picking MacIntosh and will have prepicked Honeycrisp.

The orchard sits on 10 beautiful acres where the apple varieties include Empire, MacIntosh and Red Delicious.

Apple Picking, Tractor Rides, Apple Cider, Kids Activities, Live Music, Pie.

Please follow our Instagram for the latest updates!

Music performances are every Saturday and Sunday (weather permitting) from 1:00pm – 4:00pm.

See You Soon!

  • Safety first. This is a working apple orchard, enjoy your time here while taking care of your own and others health and safety (entering orchard at own risk).
  • Admission is $2.00 per person, children under 2 are free.
  • No ladders in the orchard.
  • We recommend bringing in your own picking bags and baskets, we do have bags for a .50 charge.
  • We are an outdoor farm, we will have outdoor washrooms.
  • Please wear proper footwear as we are a working farm.
  • Please watch for fallen apples when walking through the aisles.
  • Please be mindful when visiting our orchard, native pollinators are active in this warm weather. Be careful of bee stings.
  • Live entertainment will start from 1:00PM – 4:00PM (weather permitting).
