About Us

Meet the remarkable team of JC Agri Orchards

Jack

Owner

Jack has created a niche business specializing in fruit orchard establishments in the Collingwood and surrounding areas. With over 30 years of experience in agriculture, he has become a recognized expert in the field. After studying at the University of Guelph, Jack gained valuable knowledge working with various food processing companies before successfully launching his own business.

If you would like to book a consultation in the Collingwood area with Jack, he can be reached at: jcagri1953@gmail.com

Ky

General Manager

Ky brings more than 10 years of experience in the fruit orchard industry. With a solid foundation in Horticulture and a specialization in Integrated Pest Management, she is captivated by the study of insects and diseases. Ky collaborates with both artisan and commercial orchards, driven by a passion for revitalizing older orchards. Her commitment lies in maintaining optimal conditions for the orchards, which ensures healthy and productive yields.

If you are interested in booking a consultation or would like to carry our apple cider product in the fall, you can reach Ky at k.jcagri@gmail.com

Paul

Operations Manager

Paul has been an invaluable member of the JC Agri Orchards team for the past three years, bringing with him a wealth of experience in carpentry. His creative vision and outstanding skills enable him to design captivating attractions at our Pick Your Own orchards. Paul has a deep passion for sharing his expertise and takes great pleasure in leading school tours during the harvest season. You can meet him at our King City location, Royal Orchards.

If you are interested in scheduling a consultation in King City or would like to arrange a school tour this fall, feel free to reach out to Paul at pb.jcagri@gmail.com
